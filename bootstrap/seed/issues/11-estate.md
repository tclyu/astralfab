Everything about this estate as a subject of study: its facts, and the decisions
derived from them. Analysis and design are stages of this work, not separate
containers — a design that forgets its analysis, or an analysis owned by a
different envelope than the design it feeds, is how estates drift.

**Facts in scope:** two public repositories owned by one person; a two-account
model (owner decides, automation writes) shared by several agents on one
machine; a protected working line with owner-only merges; a supply repository
whose role is not yet designed; documentation governed by a provisional
convention awaiting its standard.

**Work in this envelope:** analysis files stating the facts and constraints as
they are; design files deriving decisions from analysis and generic research;
and, as design concludes, the filing of deliverable envelopes under `Delivery`.
All of it lands as files by pull request citing this issue; the issue body holds
only scope and the current decision.
