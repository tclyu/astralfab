Everything preserved about what this estate is and how it was built — as final state and operations, not as a diary.

**Contents, all landing under this envelope:**

- `.gitignore` and `.staging/README.md` — the staging law, committed so every clone and worktree inherits both the rule and its explanation.
- `bootstrap/estate.md` — the estate declaratively: accounts, repositories, protection, tracker, conventions. What exists, never how it came to exist.
- `bootstrap/operations.md` — the ordered operations with actor and channel, the bootstrap-window enumeration, the value flips (settings that had to transit a wrong value and the condition that flipped them), discovered constraints, and the irreducible human acts.
- `bootstrap/token-ledger.md` — the measured cost of delegated execution.

The record's test: someone who was not here can read it and build the estate again — using `Scaffolding` for the mechanical part — without reconstructing any history from transcripts.
