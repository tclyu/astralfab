Container for the artifacts the estate ships. Children now: `Record` (what this
estate is and how it was built) and `Scaffolding` (the reusable mechanism that
can build another one).

Deliberately not pre-created: issues for future deliverables (skills, code,
release machinery). Each is filed here as its own single-concept envelope when
`Estate` design produces it — a placeholder bucket created before its content is
known would be a categorization error, and this container's history should show
that it never made one.
