Container for how the family is governed: the doctrines that bind every act in the estate. Children: `Method` (how work proceeds), `Authorization` (whose decision permits which act), `Identity` (which account acts, and how that is proven).

A doctrine binds once its body is accepted by the owner. The acceptance of all three closes the bootstrap window enumerated in `bootstrap/operations.md`; every act after that point is a governed act.

Everything about governance belongs in this container or its children. A new governance concern starts as a section in the closest existing body and becomes its own issue only when that body demonstrably hosts independent work streams (split-on-bloat, recorded in both bodies).
