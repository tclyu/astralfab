Everything documentation: how this estate writes things down, the research that justifies it, and the standard that results. One container by design — a "documentation convention" issue beside a "documentation" issue would make everything that is documentation but not convention silently ambiguous.

**The loop this container resolves.** To write research you need a documentation convention; to design a documentation convention you need research on documentation. Resolution, in order, all inside this envelope:

1. **Convention v0** (`docs/documentation/convention-v0.md`): provisional and deliberately minimal — what a research file must state: subject, sources, claims separated from facts, confidence, date. Its authority is the bootstrap window; its stated expiry is supersession by v1.
2. **Research on documentation practice**, written under v0 and preserved in the repository. The research that justifies the standard is itself written under the provisional convention — that is the loop paying out, not a defect.
3. **Standard v1**, designed from that evidence, supersedes v0. Files written under v0 are re-graded — promoted to v1 or kept with their provisional marker as provenance. Nothing is erased.

Each step lands by its own pull request citing this issue.
