Everything about the artifacts repository's configuration.

**Decided.** Identical to `astralfab` in branches, protection, and merge shape (see that issue), with one deliberate divergence: **issues are off**. The family has a single tracker, and disabling issues here is what makes that statement a mechanism rather than a convention. Anything concerning this repository is tracked on the `astralfab` tracker carrying the `repo:astralfab-artifacts` label.

The repository's role in the family — what it stores, in what format, and what publishes to it — is not configuration and is deliberately not decided here; it is `Estate` design work, and its outcome will be filed under `Delivery` as its own envelope when reached.
